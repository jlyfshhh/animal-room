Why physical testing matters

Similar labels can hide different hardware.

Other ways to help.

A clear bug report, a setup story, or a well-placed link can be more useful than a tip.

⭐

Star or watch the projects

Stars help other keepers find the source. Watching releases keeps you updated without a mailing list. Visit the GitHub projects →

🧪

Test a release

Try the documented install on hardware you already own, then report the exact model, OS, and result in the appropriate repository.

📝

Report a reproducible problem

Share what you expected, what happened, and the built-in diagnostic report.

📷

Share a real setup

Use the privacy-conscious setup story form to share what worked and what did not. It asks separately before anything may be featured. You can also follow or tag @thebioactivekeeper on Instagram.
