What Viv does with your data

Privacy

Short, because the truthful version is short: nothing leaves your device. This is the same policy shown inside the app, word for word.

Nothing is collected

Viv does not collect, transmit or sell any information about you or your animals. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

Everything stays on your device

Animals, care plans, history, weights, sheds, notes and photographs are stored on your iPhone. They are included in your device backups if you have those switched on.

Photographs

Choosing a photograph copies it into Viv’s own storage. Viv asks for access to your library only at the moment you pick one, and reads nothing else.

Reminders

If reminders are switched on, they are scheduled by iOS on your device. No reminder passes through any server.

Deleting your data

Deleting an animal removes its records. Deleting Viv from your iPhone removes everything it holds. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because nothing was sent anywhere else.

Children

Viv is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

If this changes

Syncing between devices is planned. If it ships, records will travel through your own iCloud account rather than a server we run, and this policy will be updated before that happens rather than after.

Getting in touch

Questions about any of this, or a request to have something explained: animalroom@pm.me, or @thebioactivekeeper on Instagram.
