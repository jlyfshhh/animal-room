Viv keeps track of the care your reptiles and amphibians need, and what actually happened. Feeding, shedding, weights and UV readings — in your pocket, at the enclosure, where the work happens.

Skipping is not the same as forgetting.

Most trackers score you on streaks. An animal in blue does not want feeding, a brumating snake does not want disturbing, and a keeper who correctly leaves them alone should not be marked down for it. Viv is built around that distinction.

What it does

The day’s care, and the history behind it

Organised around individual animals, with enough structure to show patterns over months rather than days.

📋

Today’s list, generated

Set a care plan once — feed every seven days, mist daily, full clean-out monthly — and Viv builds the day’s list from it. Nothing to maintain by hand.

📈

Weight and sheds over time

A plateau, a slow loss, or a run of patchy sheds is easy to miss week to week and obvious on a chart.

💡

UV index, taken seriously

Set a target UVI range per animal and record what your meter actually reads. Presets are starting points, not prescriptions.

👪

A household, not a database

Record who does what. If the misting is someone’s job and there is pocket money attached, Viv keeps the tally.

🔔

One quiet reminder

An evening nudge naming what is still outstanding — not one buzz per task. A phone that buzzes six times at breakfast gets its notifications turned off.

📦

Yours to take with you

Everything stays on your iPhone. Export a full backup whenever you like. There is no account to close and no service to shut down.

The part worth knowing

Four ways a task ends

Care that did not need doing is not a failure, and Viv will not pretend otherwise.

Done

It happened. Counts towards the husbandry score.

Refused

You offered; they said no. Still counts as care given — the refusal is a husbandry fact worth keeping on its own.

Skipped

It genuinely did not need doing — in blue, brumating, fed yesterday. Leaves the score entirely.

Missed

It should have happened and did not. Counts against the score, because that is the point of recording it.

Help

Questions keepers ask

Where are my records kept?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. Viv has no account and no server. That also means they are not backed up anywhere on their own — use More → Backup to export a copy, and keep your iPhone backups switched on.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Not yet. Syncing through your own iCloud account is planned. Until then each device keeps its own records.

What does the husbandry score mean?

The share of accountable care that actually happened, over the last thirty days. Work you deliberately skipped is left out of it entirely. Work you missed stays in. Today’s outstanding tasks are not counted until the day is over, so opening the app at breakfast does not show you a low score built from work you still have hours to do.

Why is my score blank?

Because there is not enough history for it to mean anything yet. A number invented on your first morning would be worse than no number.

Is the lighting guidance advice?

No. The UV index presets are common starting points for common species, not prescriptions. Check your own animal against a current care sheet and your own meter, and talk to an exotics vet about anything health related.

How is this different from Shed?

Shed is the free, open-source, self-hosted version that runs on your own server and is shared across a household on your home network. Viv is a separate iPhone app that needs no server at all. They are different tools for different setups, and Shed is not going anywhere.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting an animal removes its records. Deleting Viv from your iPhone removes everything it holds. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because nothing was sent anywhere else.
